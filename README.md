# Mash-doom-with-Necrodancer-for-VR

Hell yeah, that sounds like an absolute banger of a game.
Imagine Doom‘s brutal, fast-paced demon-slaying mayhem fused with Crypt of the NecroDancer‘s rhythm mechanics, all in VR. You’d be the Doomguy (or a dancing variant) tearing through hellish dungeons where every move, shot, reload, and dodge has to hit the beat. Miss the rhythm and your chainsaw swing whiffs, enemies get free hits, or your BFG charge fizzles. Nail the groove and you’re an unstoppable god of metal—multikills sync to the drop, glory kills trigger sick animations timed to the music, and the whole arena pulses with the beat.MFzpO“LARGE”
Core Gameplay Loop
	•	Movement & Combat on Beat: Grid-based like NecroDancer for tactical positioning, but VR freedom lets you physically step/dodge in rhythm while aiming freely (or with snap turns for accessibility). Shoot, melee, or use abilities only on the beat for max damage/ multipliers.
	•	Doom Vibes: Procedural hell levels with classic demons (Imps, Mancubi, Cyberdemons) that have attack patterns synced to the music. Pick up power-ups that change the soundtrack or add layers (e.g., a quad damage that drops the bass harder).
	•	VR Immersion: Feel the thump in your chest with haptic feedback on every perfect shot. Headbang to trigger taunts, physically swing for melee glory kills, or duck to avoid a Revenant rocket on the off-beat.
	•	Soundtrack: Danny Baranowsky-style chiptunes mixed with Mick Gordon’s heavy metal riffs. Custom track support so you can import your own metal/electronic playlists.
